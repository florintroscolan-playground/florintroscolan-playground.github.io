E-learning for small business

A learning platform and its brand, designed from zero for people starting or running a small business.

  • Platform and brand
  • Brand identity
  • Admin tool
  • Mobile web

2 min read

At a glance

Challenge

A platform built from zero, brand included, for people starting or running a small business.

Approach

A review of 11 e-learning platforms, three homepage wireframe options, then the UI and the admin.

Outcome

Full platform designed end to end: public site, learning area, checkout and course admin.

The problem

Where it started

  1. 01

    Nothing existed yet

    No product and no brand: the identity, public site, learning area and admin all started from zero.

  2. 02

    Practical, local topics

    Courses cover setting up a company, online resources, org charts, the tax authority and European funds.

  3. 03

    Two ways to pay

    Subscriptions with a free trial sit next to paid one-to-one consultancy, and both had to read clearly.

Process

How it ran

  1. 01 Discover A review of 11 e-learning platforms, with pros and cons.
  2. 02 Structure Three homepage wireframe options, each with a different lead.
  3. 03 Explore Hero explorations: a split collage and two centred photo heroes.
  4. 04 Design & system Wireframe to final screens, built on a reusable course card.
  5. 05 Deliver Desktop and mobile web, plus the course admin.

Key decisions

Four calls that shaped it

01

Browse first, sign up later

Courses and lessons can be seen without an account, and a locked lesson shows what the free trial unlocks. The competitor review pointed to this pattern.

Details
Trade-off
Every locked lesson needs its own upsell state.
02

Sign-in as a modal

Sign-in and register open over the current page, so a visitor keeps their place. Full pages were designed as well, for direct links.

Details
Trade-off
Two versions of each form to keep in step.
03

One centred hero after three directions

The wireframes tried a promo hero, a video hero and a consultancy-led hero. The final homepage uses a centred hero with a strip of topics under it.

Details
Rejected
A split hero with a photo collage.
04

Pricing modelled on subscription leaders

A monthly or yearly toggle, three plans with the middle one highlighted and a free trial, with consultancy sold separately.

Outcome

What was designed

  • Public site

    Homepage, courses, pricing, about, FAQ and contact.

  • Learning area

    Library, lesson pages with chapters and locked-lesson upsells.

  • Checkout and account

    Card or PayPal checkout, account settings and notifications.

  • Course admin

    Course details, publishing, adding videos and drag-to-reorder.
