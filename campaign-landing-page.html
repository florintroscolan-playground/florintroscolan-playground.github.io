Campaign for small worries

A campaign landing page and a month of social content that sell peace of mind, not devices.

  • AI-assisted campaign
  • Landing page
  • Content plan
  • AI-generated visuals

3 min read

At a glance

Challenge

Make smart-home devices feel like relief from everyday worries, not more technology to manage.

Approach

AI to explore more ideas, a page built around moments, then an interactive build coded in Antigravity.

Outcome

A desktop and mobile landing page and a June plan of posts, carousels and stories. Published in June 2026.

The problem

Where it started

  1. 01

    Devices are not the point

    People do not want more gadgets; they want to stop going back to check the door.

  2. 02

    Many products, one story

    Cameras, sensors, plugs, bulbs and a hub had to sit inside everyday moments.

  3. 03

    A month of content to fill

    June needed posts, carousels, stories and ad sizes around one idea.

Process

How it ran

My part was the landing page and the June content; other months and the key visuals were by others.

  1. 01 Explore Images with ChatGPT, video with the KIE API, edits in Adobe.
  2. 02 Structure A block layout: hero, insight, scenarios, products, CTA.
  3. 03 Design Desktop at 1440 and mobile at 393, built from components.
  4. 04 Prototype Hover hotspots, card states and tabs, then coded in Antigravity.
  5. 05 Content June posts, carousels, stories with polls, and 26 ad resizes.

Key decisions

Three calls that shaped it

01

Lead with moments, not products

The page opens on small worries and sorts the range by time of day. Products appear as chips inside each scene.
