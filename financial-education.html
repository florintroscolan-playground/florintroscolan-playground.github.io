At a glance

Challenge

After years of growth, 11 courses, 166 articles and 364 worksheets sat on pages that no longer matched.

Approach

A full site audit cut the navigation to 5 sections and moved every page onto 15 templates.

Outcome

Launched on the fixed date. The bank’s team now adds courses and events from 15 templates.

Final design

The problem

Where it started

  1. 01

    Many pages, few real page types

    52 pages, mostly variations of a handful of layouts, each one built from scratch.

  2. 02

    A menu that mirrored the bank, not visitors

    7 pages reachable only through side links, a duplicate contact page and expired 2023 events in the navigation.

  3. 03

    Tablets got a stretched phone layout

    The desktop breakpoint appeared 275 times in the code, the tablet one only 22.

Key decisions

Three calls that shaped it

01

Templates before pages

The audit showed 52 pages but only a handful of real page types. 15 templates, each mapped to its pages, let development start while design continued.

Details
Rejected
Redesigning page by page, repeating the inconsistency the audit found.
Trade-off
A few pages, like the homepage, kept bespoke templates on purpose.
02

Five top-level sections

Navigation mirrored internal programmes. It now follows what people come to do: courses, programmes, resources, teachers and about. Overlapping pages merged, with 301 redirects.

Details
Rejected
Programme names at the top level, clear to the bank, not visitors.
Trade-off
Some programmes moved into a mega menu with short descriptions.
03

One filter pattern everywhere

Two listings switched to touch filters at different breakpoints. One rule now applies: inline filters on desktop, a bottom sheet below 1024 px.

Details
Rejected
Inline filters from 640 px up, cramped on tablets.
Trade-off
Tablets lose inline filters but get one touch-first pattern.

Outcome

What the team got

  • 52→15 Pages consolidated into reusable page templates
  • 19 Badges in the gamification layer, each tied to learning
  • 5 Client demos signed off, launch date never moved
